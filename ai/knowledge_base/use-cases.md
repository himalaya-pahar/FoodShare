# FoodShare Workflows

Use these role-based steps to answer questions about what to do in the app.

## Restaurant: post food

1. Sign in as an approved Restaurant.
2. Open the donation creation screen.
3. Enter the food, quantity, preparation time, pickup deadline, area, and address.
4. Add optional storage, allergen, image, or video information.
5. Submit the donation. It becomes Available for NGOs.

## NGO: find food

1. Sign in as an approved NGO.
2. Open the available donations list.
3. Filter by area when useful.
4. Open a donation to review its quantity, deadline, location, and notes.

## NGO: request a pickup

1. Open an Available donation.
2. Choose the pickup request option.
3. Enter an estimated pickup time within the donation's pickup window.
4. Add an optional message.
5. Submit and wait for the restaurant's decision.

## Restaurant: review requests

1. Open one of your donations.
2. Review its pending pickup requests.
3. Accept one request or reject a request.
4. Accepting one request reserves the donation and rejects other pending requests.

## NGO: collect food

1. Wait until the restaurant accepts your request.
2. Collect the food at the agreed place and time.
3. Mark the pickup Collected after receiving it.

## Restaurant: complete the handoff

1. Wait for the NGO to mark the pickup Collected.
2. Confirm that the food was handed over.
3. Mark the donation Completed.

## Account approval

1. Register as a Restaurant or NGO.
2. Wait while an administrator reviews the account.
3. Sign in after approval.

## When something goes wrong

Check the donation status, pickup deadline, account approval status, and your role. Ask the restaurant or administrator for help when the issue requires their decision.
