# What FoodShare Does

FoodShare helps restaurants share surplus food with NGOs. Restaurants post available food, NGOs request pickups, and both sides follow the handoff status.

## Roles

- Restaurants post and manage their donations, review pickup requests, and confirm completed handoffs.
- NGOs browse available donations, request pickups, withdraw pending requests, and mark accepted pickups as collected.
- Administrators review accounts, manage users, and inspect activity history.

## Main journey

1. A person signs up as a Restaurant or NGO.
2. An administrator reviews the account.
3. An approved restaurant posts surplus food.
4. An NGO browses available food and requests a pickup.
5. The restaurant accepts or rejects the request.
6. The NGO collects the food and marks it Collected.
7. The restaurant confirms the handoff and marks it Completed.

## Donation information

Donations can include the food name, description, quantity, unit, prepared time, pickup deadline, area, address, storage notes, allergen information, images, and a video.

## Safety and privacy

FoodShare limits actions by role. Users should share only the information needed for a donation or pickup. The assistant explains visible app behavior and does not reveal private technical or security information.
