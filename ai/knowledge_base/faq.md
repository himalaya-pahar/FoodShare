# FoodShare Questions and Answers

Answer these common questions directly and in plain language.

### Why cannot I log in after signing up?
New accounts need administrator approval first. Once an administrator approves the account, try logging in again.

### Why is my account still pending?
An administrator has not finished reviewing it yet. You can sign in after the account is approved.

### What should I do if my account was rejected?
Contact the administrator for clarification. Only an administrator can review the account decision.

### Can I register as a donor?
Yes. In FoodShare, restaurants register as the role that posts surplus food for NGOs.

### Which roles can sign up?
People can sign up as a Restaurant or NGO. Administrator accounts are created separately.

### How does an NGO request food?
Open an Available donation, choose the pickup option, enter an estimated pickup time within the donation's pickup window, and submit the request.

### How can an NGO claim or reserve food?
Open an Available donation and submit a pickup request. The donation becomes Reserved only after the restaurant accepts your request.

### What information is needed to request a pickup?
You need an estimated pickup time that fits the donation's pickup window. You may also add a message for the restaurant.

### Why was my pickup request not accepted?
The restaurant may have rejected it, or another NGO's request may have been accepted first. Check the request status or contact the restaurant.

### Can I request food after the deadline?
No. A pickup request must use a time within the donation's pickup window, and an expired donation is no longer available.

### What happens after a pickup request?
The request waits for the restaurant. The restaurant can accept or reject it. If another request is accepted first, pending requests for the same donation are rejected automatically.

### What happens when the restaurant accepts my request?
The donation becomes Reserved, your request is accepted, and other pending requests for that donation are rejected.

### What happens when the restaurant rejects my request?
Your request becomes rejected. The donation can remain Available for other NGOs unless its status changes.

### What does Available mean?
The restaurant has posted the donation and NGOs can request it.

### What does Reserved mean?
The restaurant accepted one NGO's request. The donation is no longer open to other pickup requests.

### What does Collected mean?
The NGO has marked the food as picked up. The restaurant still needs to confirm the handoff.

### What does Completed mean?
The restaurant confirmed that the food was handed over successfully.

### What does Cancelled mean?
The restaurant cancelled the donation while it was still Available. It is no longer open for pickup requests.

### What does Expired mean?
The pickup deadline passed before the donation was completed. It is no longer available for a new pickup request.

### Can a restaurant change an accepted donation?
No. Donation details can be edited or cancelled only while the donation is Available.

### Can an NGO request the same donation twice?
No. Each NGO can have only one request for a donation.

### Can I change a donation after someone requests it?
A restaurant can still edit or cancel a donation while it is Available. Once it is Reserved, its details cannot be changed.

### Can I withdraw a pickup request?
Yes, an NGO can withdraw its own request while it is still pending.

### When should I mark a pickup as collected?
Mark it Collected after you physically receive the food from the restaurant.

### Who marks a donation completed?
The restaurant marks it Completed after confirming that the food was handed over.

### Where can I see status changes?
Activity history shows status changes and when they happened. The history is for viewing and cannot be edited.

### What files can I add?
Donations support up to five images and one video. Images can be JPEG, PNG, or WebP. Videos can be MP4. Each file can be up to 5 MB.

### Can I add photos of donated food?
Yes. A restaurant can add up to five JPEG, PNG, or WebP images to a donation.

### Can I add a video to a donation?
Yes. A restaurant can add one MP4 video, up to 5 MB.

### How many photos can a donation have?
A donation can have up to five images and one video.

### Can I add a profile picture?
Yes. Users can add one profile image in JPEG, PNG, or WebP format, up to 5 MB.

### Can I delete my account?
There is no self-service account deletion. An administrator can remove an inactive non-administrator account.

### Can I filter donations by location?
Yes. NGOs can filter available donations by area to find food near them.

### What can a restaurant include in a donation?
A restaurant can provide the food name, description, quantity, unit, preparation time, pickup deadline, area, address, storage notes, allergen information, images, and video.

### What if I enter an invalid quantity?
The quantity must be greater than zero. Correct it before submitting the donation.

### What if my pickup time is outside the allowed window?
Choose a time between the food's preparation time and pickup deadline.

### Can the assistant perform an action for me?
No. The assistant only explains FoodShare and cannot change accounts, donations, pickup requests, or statuses.
