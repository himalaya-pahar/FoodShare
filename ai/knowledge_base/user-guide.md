# FoodShare User Guide

## Getting started

Choose Sign up, select Restaurant or NGO, enter the requested account details, and submit. Your account must be approved by an administrator before you can sign in.

## Signing in

Open Login and enter the email and password used for the account. If the account is still waiting for approval, wait for the administrator's decision.

## Restaurant guide

Restaurants can create donations with food details, quantity, preparation time, pickup deadline, area, address, storage notes, allergen information, images, and video. Only Available donations can be edited or cancelled.

To handle requests, open one of your donations and review its pending requests. Accepting one request reserves the donation and rejects the other pending requests. After the NGO collects the food, confirm the handoff as Completed.

## NGO guide

NGOs can browse Available donations and filter them by area. Open a donation to review its details, then request a pickup with an estimated time inside the pickup window. An NGO can have only one request per donation.

An NGO may withdraw its own pending request. After acceptance, collect the food and mark the pickup Collected.

## Administrator guide

Administrators review pending accounts, approve or reject them, view users, and remove inactive non-administrator accounts. Administrators can also inspect activity history.

## Donation statuses

Available means open for requests. Reserved means a request was accepted. Collected means the NGO marked the food picked up. Completed means the restaurant confirmed the handoff. Cancelled means the restaurant withdrew the donation. Expired means its pickup deadline passed.

## Media

Donations can include up to five images and one video. Supported image formats are JPEG, PNG, and WebP. The supported video format is MP4. Each file can be up to 5 MB.
